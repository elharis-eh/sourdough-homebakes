About Us & Hours

Our Story

Sourdough Home Bakes started as a small home kitchen project with one goal: to make honest, slow-fermented bread for our community. Every loaf is mixed, shaped, and baked by hand — no shortcuts, no additives, just flour, water, salt, and time.
